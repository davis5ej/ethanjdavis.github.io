Eugenia Cheng introduces category theory to readers with no formal mathematical background. She starts from everyday examples such as privilege and driving routes and builds gradually to the formal machinery: categories, functors, natural transformations, duality, and a glimpse of higher-dimensional category theory. Throughout, she argues that abstraction is a tool for understanding, both in math and in life.
