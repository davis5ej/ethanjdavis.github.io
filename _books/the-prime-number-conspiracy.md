This collection of articles from *Quanta Magazine*, edited by Thomas Lin, covers major recent developments in mathematics for a general audience. The title piece reports the 2016 discovery that the last digits of consecutive prime numbers are biased in a way no one expected. Other articles cover work on the gaps between primes and range widely across number theory, geometry, and infinity, along with the mathematicians behind the results.
