Matt Parker collects real-world cases of math going wrong: a footbridge that wobbled when it wasn't supposed to, a building that shook because a gym class jumped in time to a pop song, and billions of dollars lost to calculation errors. For each one he explains the math behind the mistake, from rounding and unit conversions to the way computers keep track of time. The book is funny throughout and a great read.
