Steven Strogatz tells the story of calculus through what he calls the Infinity Principle: to understand something complicated, cut it into infinitely many simple pieces, solve those, and put them back together. He traces the idea from Archimedes through Galileo, Kepler, Newton, and Leibniz to modern applications, explaining derivatives, integrals, and differential equations in plain language along the way. The book is as much about why calculus works as about its history.
